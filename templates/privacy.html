Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects only the information necessary to provide our expense tracking service. This includes:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and password (hashed and securely stored).
  • Expense Data: Amount, date, category, description, and any notes you enter for each expense.
  • Preferences: Your selected currency, default categories, and display settings.
  • Device Information: Browser type and version when you access the service, to help us improve compatibility.

2. How We Use Your Data

All data collected is used solely to provide and enhance the Spendly service:

  • To store and display your expenses for personal financial tracking.
  • To generate analytics and summaries about your spending patterns.
  • To personalize your experience based on your preferences.
  • To communicate important service updates or security notices (if you opt in to notifications).
  • To prevent fraudulent or abusive activity.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers using industry-standard encryption and security measures. We use a secure database connection and encrypt data at rest where possible.

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active. If you choose to delete your account or request data deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, though aggregated and anonymized data may be retained for analytical purposes.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may integrate with third-party services to enhance functionality:

  • Authentication Providers: We may use third-party authentication (e.g., OAuth) if you sign in via Google or other providers.
  • Hosting and Infrastructure: Your data may be stored on cloud infrastructure providers.
  • Analytics: We may use anonymized analytics to understand service usage and improve the product.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Any third-party services we use are contractually obligated to maintain the confidentiality and security of your data.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, or if you wish to exercise your data rights (access, correction, deletion), please contact us:

Email: privacy@spendly.app
Website: spendly.app

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. If you have an active account, we may also notify you via email about significant changes.
